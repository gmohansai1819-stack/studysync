Project overview

One clear workspace for everyday academic planning.

StudySync is a student planner website designed to bring everyday academic planning into one place. The goal is to make study planning simpler and help students focus on what matters.

The problem

Students often need to manage assignments, class schedules, deadlines, and goals separately. This can make it harder to stay organized.

The solution

StudySync brings tasks, schedules, upcoming deadlines, and weekly goals together in a single, calm interface.

One workspace • clearer priorities • steady progress

Main features

Tasks, schedule, and goals.

Designed to support a balanced and manageable study routine.

Plan tasks

Break assignments into smaller steps, set priorities, and mark tasks as completed.

Schedule

View classes, study sessions, and important dates together.

Track progress

Set weekly study goals and monitor how consistently you study.

How StudySync works

Small steps make academic work feel more manageable.

  1. Add tasks

    Enter assignments, reminders, and things to remember.

  2. Plan your week

    View your study time and important dates together.

  3. Track progress

    Check off completed tasks and review weekly goals.
